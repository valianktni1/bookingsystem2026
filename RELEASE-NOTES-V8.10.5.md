# BookingSystem2026 V8.10.5

Build: `2026.08.19-client-email-centre-v8.10.5`

## Email client from every record

- A clear **Email client** button is always visible in the booking header on
  desktop and mobile.
- The same action is also available beside Call, Directions and Client area.
- Choose an existing active email template or write a manual one-off email.
- The recipient, subject and full wording are visible before anything is sent.
- Template wording can be adjusted for one email without altering the saved
  master template.
- Every sent message receives a newly generated, booking-specific secure client
  account link automatically.
- The composer recommends **Contract reminder** when the Wedding Booking Form is
  complete but the agreement has been reset or is not yet signed.

## Safety

- Opening the composer or deploying this version sends nothing.
- A cancelled booking must be reopened before an email can be sent.
- Imported Studio Ninja clients remain manual-only. A reason and the exact phrase
  `SEND ONE MANUAL EMAIL` are required for each deliberate message.
- Sending a one-off imported-client email never enables automatic messages or
  reminders.
- Existing bookings, agreements, invoices, payments, invoice numbers and Accounts
  integration settings are unchanged.
- All 31 cumulative automated tests pass.
