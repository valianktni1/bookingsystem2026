# BookingSystem2026 V8.23 - Complete Final Call Pack

## What changed

- Every wedding Journey now includes a private Step 5 Final Call Pack.
- It combines the wedding record, contact details, venues, package, Wedding
  Booking Form, Final Wedding Timings, run sheet, coverage warnings, agreement,
  invoices and payment position.
- A ten-point checklist and editable private notes can be saved during the call.
- **Mark final call complete** completes the existing private telephone task.
- A completed call can be reopened without losing its checklist or notes.
- One professionally formatted PDF is refreshed and retained privately in Files.
- The Today final-call queue opens directly at this pack.

## Safety preserved

- No new automatic or manual client email is sent by this feature.
- The pack is administrator-only and is never shown in the client portal.
- Studio Ninja bookings can use it without removing their automation suppression.
- Existing bookings, invoices, agreements, forms, Calendar and Accounts settings
  are not rewritten.
