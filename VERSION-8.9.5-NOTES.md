# BookingSystem2026 V8.9.5 - Clean Admin Workspace

This complete cumulative deployment refines the photographer/admin experience without changing the client journey.

## Booking command centre

- Full-page record with a compact, sticky command header.
- Back, Edit, Cancel/Reopen, Email, Call, Directions and Client area are immediately available.
- More actions contains Archive and the protected Delete test / duplicate control.
- Six concise summary cards provide the important booking and financial position at a glance.

## Progressive workflow

Every booking uses the same section order:

1. Overview
2. Quote & emails
3. Payments
4. Forms & agreement
5. Files
6. Notes & history

The wedding progress bar follows Enquiry, Quote, Payment, Booking form, Agreement and Wedding. Each stage opens its relevant section.

## Cancellation, voids and test records

- Cancel booking is visible at the top of every active record.
- Cancelled records clearly show their cancellation reason and can be reopened.
- Voided invoices remain visible with a VOID banner and £0 outstanding balance.
- A zero-payment test or duplicate may be deleted even when its test invoice was voided.
- Deleted test invoice numbers remain consumed and are never reused.
- Any genuine payment permanently protects the booking from deletion.
- Studio Ninja imports remain protected, manual-communication-only records.

## Safety

- No destructive database migration is required.
- Deployment sends no client emails.
- Existing bookings, files, contracts, payments and invoice counters are preserved.
- The client portal and V8.9.4 email/template improvements are unchanged.
