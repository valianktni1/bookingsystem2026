# BookingSystem2026 V8.8 - Required Quote Items & Private Discounts

V8.8 is cumulative and contains every feature and safety control from V8.7.1.

## New quote preparation

- Select a normal add-on, override its price for that wedding and make it required.
- Required items are shown to the couple already included and cannot be removed.
- Change or remove prepared items safely until the quote is accepted.
- Accepted quote and invoice preserve the exact item, wording and amount.

## Private discount codes

- Add reusable Private discount codes in Packages & pricing.
- Private codes never appear as client-selectable add-ons.
- Apply a code and override its deduction while preparing an individual quote.
- The couple sees only the applied deduction and correct reduced total.
- Accepted invoices retain the discount as a negative line item.

## Safety

- Existing quotes, invoices and Studio Ninja imports are unchanged.
- Accepted quote preparation is locked.
- Catalog items used by an accepted quote remain protected from deletion.
- The database change is additive and runs automatically during startup.
