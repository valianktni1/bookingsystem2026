# BookingSystem2026 - Version 8.3

## Complete enquiry, quote and payment journey

- A secure wedding area and long-lived private link are created with every website enquiry.
- The acknowledgement email contains that link while the couple waits for their quote.
- Before accepting a package, the couple sees only their enquiry and package choices.
- Package acceptance creates the invoice and unlocks the Wedding Booking Form/questionnaire and contract.
- The acceptance email links to the same wedding area and asks the couple to complete the form and digitally sign the contract.
- The deposit remains due one day after acceptance and the final balance remains due 45 days before the wedding.
- Any first bank-transfer payment secures the date, including a part-payment of the expected deposit.
- Every payment reduces the displayed balance and sends a branded confirmation containing the amount received, total paid, remaining balance, due date, wedding-area link and receipt access.
- Payment saving is independent of email delivery: if SMTP fails, the payment remains safely recorded and Mark sees a clear warning.
- All Version 8.2 safe cancellation, voiding, reset and deletion controls and Version 8.2.1 mobile readability changes are retained.
