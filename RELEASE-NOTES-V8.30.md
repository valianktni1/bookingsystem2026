# BookingSystem2026 V8.30

Build: `2026.08.26-studio-style-workspace-v8.30`

## What visibly changed

- The desktop client Overview is now a Studio-Ninja-inspired working screen rather than a long summary page.
- The left column shows the wedding workflow and the couple's recent sent/received email conversation.
- The right column shows Job and Client details plus direct cards for invoices, quote, agreement, questionnaires, files, notes and tasks.
- Wedding Bookings opens on **Upcoming weddings** in date order.
- **All bookings** sits immediately beside it and retains completed and cancelled records.
- Past active weddings appear in a clear **Past weddings awaiting completion** queue.
- Active weddings have a deliberate **Mark wedding complete** control.
- Completed weddings can be reopened if clicked accidentally.

## Completion safety

Marking a wedding complete:

- does not delete it;
- does not archive it;
- does not send the couple an email;
- retains invoices, payments, forms, agreements, files, notes, tasks and activity history;
- records who completed it and when;
- removes it from Upcoming weddings but keeps it under All bookings.

## Existing protections retained

- Studio Ninja general automatic emails remain blocked.
- Quote emails still require your review and deliberate send.
- Accounts synchronisation remains controlled by your existing settings.
- V8.29 durable form-draft recovery is included.
- V8.28.4.2 receipt-link and inline invoice protections are included.
- Complete-backup support remains included.
