# BookingSystem2026 V8.8.2 - Admin Questionnaires

V8.8.2 is cumulative and includes every feature and safety control from V8.8.1.

## Dedicated record section

- Every wedding record now includes a clear Questionnaires section.
- New BookingSystem2026 Wedding Booking Forms are shown as readable question-and-answer rows.
- Final wedding details questionnaires appear separately when submitted.
- Long answers retain their original formatting and line breaks.
- Second venues, ceremony details, reception details, timings, special requests, music and supplier information are all visible.
- Ivory Digital project information uses the same safe read-only view.

## Studio Ninja imports

- Imported structured questionnaire answers are displayed from the existing migration data.
- The complete original questionnaire text can be expanded and read.
- The retained original Studio Ninja questionnaire PDF can be downloaded from the same section.
- Nothing is re-imported, altered or emailed.

## Still included

- Required travel and other mandatory quote add-ons.
- Admin-only reusable discount codes.
- Final balance dates calculated 45 days before each wedding.
- Payment register due-date sorting and overdue highlighting.
- Back to bookings beside Email, Call and Directions on every full record page.
