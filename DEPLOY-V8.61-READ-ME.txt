WHAT CHANGED
- Fixed a booking-opening wrapper that discarded the dashboard action.
- Review quote acceptance opens saved quote details, not a payment entry form.
- Review signed agreement opens its PDF. Reviewing an agreement does not
  countersign it or email anyone; existing explicit countersign controls remain.
- Review submitted booking form opens readable answers directly, with explicit
  mark-reviewed action when needed. Review timings opens the existing full form.
- Native booking header has View quote and Original enquiry buttons.
- Saved quote shows package contents, extras, discounts, total and acceptance.
  Unaccepted quotes link to the existing read-only offered-quote preview.
- Quote panels no longer repeat email delivery, email history and conversation.
  Those remain under Messages. General link/send and reset controls are folded.
- Mobile: readable text, wrapping buttons, 44px primary tap targets, no sideways
  scrolling in the checked quote/booking-form modals.

ORIGINAL ENQUIRY
The authenticated read-only endpoint shows the saved website submission.
The booking-form save previously replaced that field; future submissions now
preserve it. For older missing snapshots, the exact saved new-enquiry-admin
notification email is shown with a clear label. If neither exists, the app
says unavailable; it never fabricates the original from current booking fields.
No bulk migration or reconstruction of historic data is performed.

PROTECTION
Native quote-panel cleanup excludes Studio Ninja imports. No accepted quote,
invoice, bank detail or protected original is rewritten. Read-only Review
opens no email/send/payment action. All existing explicit save/send controls
retain their safeguards. Includes V8.60 position memory and earlier updates.
