# BookingSystem2026 V8.32

Build: `2026.08.28-friendly-workspace-v8.32`

## A genuinely friendly mobile workspace

- Important phone text is now 15–19px rather than the previous 8–11px labels.
- Dashboard shortcuts are large two-column tiles with readable action queues below.
- Upcoming Weddings and All Bookings remain directly at the top.
- Every wedding is a large card showing the couple, package, venue, date, status and outstanding balance.
- Opening a couple puts six direct actions under their name: **Email**, **Call**, **Invoice**, **Questionnaire**, **Notes** and **More**.
- The mobile screen shows four essential facts and one next action instead of a long horizontal workflow strip.
- Detailed areas remain large stacked sections; there are no sideways section tabs.
- Buttons, forms, invoices and questionnaire answers use larger touch-friendly text.

## Clearer desktop workspace

- The wedding list, workflow, recent mail, client details and booking summary use larger readable text.
- The same six direct actions appear beneath the couple header.
- Package and outstanding balance are visible in the main Wedding Bookings list.
- Existing detailed controls remain available without duplicating the whole job dashboard.

## Safety retained

V8.31.1 date-clash warnings, V8.31 email opening/link evidence, Studio Ninja manual-communication protections, invoices, receipts, backups and Google Calendar behaviour remain unchanged. Accounts synchronisation remains off.
