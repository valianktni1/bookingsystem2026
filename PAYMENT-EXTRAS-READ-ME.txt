PAYMENTS: BOOKED ADD-ONS / EXTRAS SUMMARY

A read-only Add-ons / extras card now appears directly beneath the Payments
invoice heading and above the existing invoice cards. It shows the booked extra
names, quantities where relevant, and saved prices. Later invoice amendments are
labelled Added later. It works on mobile and desktop, in light and dark themes.

The summary reads retained invoice line items. It includes paid invoices, but
excludes voided/cancelled invoices, discounts, negative charges and zero quantities.
A zero-priced separately booked extra is shown as No extra charge. Missing prices
are shown as Price not recorded rather than a made-up zero.

If an invoice has no itemised lines, the linked accepted quote can supply them.
Invoice lines take priority over quote lines to prevent duplicate or stale extras.
Quotes for another invoice and unaccepted quote offers are not used.

For imports without type labels, separately named photobooth, album, extra-hour
and certain other recognisable extras are displayed. Separate extra-only invoices,
such as Album add-on, are also recognised. The summary does not scan a package's
included-features description, infer extras from its total or consult current
catalogue prices. If older records contain no itemised extras, it says so rather
than claiming that none were chosen. Two separate invoices remain two sources,
with invoice references shown when needed.

No invoice data, totals, payments, PDFs, email contents, schedules or existing
invoice action controls are changed. The update changes only static assets and
adds a read-only wrapper around the existing Payments renderer.

Validation: 18 invoice/quote/import scenarios passed. The browser test compares
the existing invoice cards before/after the new summary and verifies unchanged
input data, existing View invoice / Record payment / New invoice handlers,
correct placement, no repeated quote extras, escaped/long names, and no horizontal
overflow at 320, 390, 768 and 1440px in light and dark themes. It performs no API
calls, writes or email sends. JavaScript syntax checking also passed.

Deploy with DEPLOY-PAYMENT-EXTRAS.sh, then reload the couple's Payments tab.
