# BookingSystem2026 V8.33 — Direct Final Wedding Timings Access

Build: `2026.08.30-final-timings-shortcut-v8.33`

## What changed

- Every wedding now shows a clear **Final timings** quick action directly beneath the couple's header on desktop and mobile.
- A submitted form opens immediately in the complete read-only answer view.
- A form that is not yet submitted opens at its existing status, early-open and send controls.
- The existing Final Timings panel is available correctly in the latest stacked mobile workspace.
- Ivory Digital projects keep their existing Questionnaire shortcut.

## Confirmed 30-day workflow

There is one combined automatic email exactly 30 days before an eligible wedding. It contains the check-in wording and a button that opens the couple's secure area directly on the Final Wedding Timings Form. A separate second timings email is not sent.

## Safety

This release changes no reminder dates, email templates, invoices, payments, Calendar behaviour, Accounts settings or Studio Ninja communication protections.
