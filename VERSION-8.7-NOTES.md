# BookingSystem2026 V8.7 — Guided Booking Workspace

V8.7 is a cumulative usability release built on the complete V8.6 system. It does not alter invoice numbers, imported records, stored documents, payment history or automation safety rules.

## Simpler navigation

- New enquiries have their own workspace for records still at enquiry or quote stage.
- Wedding bookings contain confirmed, in-progress, completed and cancelled weddings.
- The sidebar is grouped into Workspace, Tools and Setup.
- Mobile navigation gives direct access to Home, Enquiries and Weddings.
- Individual bookings use the same large expandable sections on desktop and mobile for client details, journey, invoices, files and notes.

## One guided wedding journey

Opening a booking now shows:

1. Enquiry
2. Quote
3. Payment
4. Forms
5. Wedding

A plain-English **Your next step** card points to the correct part of the booking. The six older record tabs have been consolidated into Overview, Journey, Payments, Files and Notes. Existing internal links to the old tab names remain compatible.

## Easier record workspace

- Wider desktop workspace and full-screen mobile workspace.
- Larger text and controls.
- Quick Email, Call and Google Maps Directions links.
- Overview shortcuts for Journey, Payments, Files and Notes.
- Private tasks are grouped at the bottom of Journey instead of occupying a separate record tab.

## Studio Ninja safety

All imported Studio Ninja weddings remain visible in confirmed totals, upcoming dates, calendars and wedding lists. Their automatic emails, reminders, payment confirmations and portal-link creation remain suppressed. V8.7 only changes how those records are presented.

## Additional booking imports

No additional booking batch is included in this release. Further imports should be handled only after the V8.7 interface has been deployed and checked.

## Enquiry form payment information

The enquiry form now presents the three available payment arrangements clearly: £100 plus the final balance, £100 plus two equal payments, or a 25% deposit plus the remaining 75%.
