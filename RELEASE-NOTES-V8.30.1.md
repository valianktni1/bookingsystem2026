# BookingSystem2026 V8.30.1

Build: `2026.08.26-studio-mobile-workspace-v8.30.1`

This is the dedicated mobile refinement based on the supplied Studio Ninja phone references.

## Phone dashboard

- Six large shortcuts for Enquiries, Weddings, Calendar, Payments, Inbox and Settings.
- Existing upcoming weddings and working queues remain directly below them.
- Permanent bottom navigation now prioritises Home, Enquiries, Weddings, Calendar and Payments.

## Phone wedding list

- Replaces the wide desktop-style table with one readable card per wedding.
- Shows couple, venue, wedding date, status and value without sideways scrolling.
- Keeps the Upcoming Weddings and All Bookings switch at the top.
- Same-date booking warnings and completion controls remain visible.

## Phone couple screen

- Compact header with Back, Email and More controls.
- Couple, venue, wedding date, package and financial summary remain immediately available.
- Large stacked sections replace the sideways desktop tabs:
  - Couple & wedding
  - Quote & mail
  - Invoices & payments
  - Agreement & forms
  - Files
  - Notes & activity

All V8.30 desktop changes and earlier safety fixes are included.
