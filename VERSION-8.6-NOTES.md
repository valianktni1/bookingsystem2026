# BookingSystem2026 V8.6 — Simple Wedding Workflow

This cumulative build replaces the oversized generic wedding task list with Mark's real working flow.

## Automatic flow for new BookingSystem2026 enquiries

- Mark sends the initial package quote.
- If it remains unaccepted, the couple receives a polite delivery check after 1 day and a final check after 9 days.
- Quote acceptance immediately creates the invoice and sends the booking confirmation with the wedding-area link.
- If no first payment is recorded, one booking-fee reminder is sent the next day.
- Any first payment secures the wedding, including a part-payment such as £50 of a £100 booking fee.
- A friendly check-in is sent 120 days before the wedding.
- Final details open and the questionnaire is sent 30 days before the wedding.
- Outstanding final balances are reminded 7 days before, 1 day before, 2 days after, and every 2 days afterwards until paid.

## A much cleaner task screen

New weddings receive only three practical tasks: send the package quote, receive the booking form and receive the signed contract. Automatic emails and payment reminders no longer create visible task clutter.

The old generated tasks attached to Studio Ninja imports are hidden from the dashboard, global workflow list and booking drawer without deleting any imported database data. A private manual task added later remains visible.

## Imported wedding safety

All imported Studio Ninja weddings remain confirmed and visible in dashboard counts, upcoming dates and calendar views. Their automatic emails, payment confirmations, reminders and automatic portal links remain permanently paused.

Google Calendar is not added, contacted or changed by this build.
