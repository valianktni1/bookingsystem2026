# Phase 2A release notes

## Existing data

Phase 2A uses additive startup migrations. Existing Phase 1 PostgreSQL records, invoice numbers, uploads and the administrator login are retained. The update adds archive, payment, note and branded PDF support without resetting either TrueNAS dataset.

## Daily record management

- Edit full wedding and digital project records, including contact information
- Change status, date, venue/project, package/service and financial dates
- Filter by status and view, archive or restore old records
- Add timestamped notes and review the activity history
- Add, edit, complete and delete workflow tasks
- Upload, download and delete PDF, DOCX, JPEG and PNG documents

## Finance

- Create brand-aware invoices using the shared chronological counter
- Record deposits, part-payments and balances received by bank transfer
- Automatically move an enquiry or quote to Confirmed when a dated deposit/payment is recorded
- Download a clean branded invoice PDF
- Download a receipt after any payment is recorded
- Configure legal/trading name, contact information and bank details separately for Weddings By Mark and Ivory Digital

## Not included yet

Email templates, client-facing forms, questionnaires, e-sign contracts, Studio Ninja import and gallery automation remain in later phases.
