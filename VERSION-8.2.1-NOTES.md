# BookingSystem2026 - Version 8.2.1

## Mobile readability and time-aware greeting

- The dashboard greeting now follows the current Europe/London time and displays Good morning, Good afternoon or Good evening.
- Supporting text, card descriptions, booking details, status labels, forms and the bottom navigation are clearer on phones.
- Desktop typography and layout remain unchanged.
- All Version 8.2 cancellation, voiding, reset and safe-deletion controls are retained.
