# BookingSystem2026 V8.28.4 - In-System Invoice Preview

Build: `2026.08.24-invoice-preview-v8.28.4`

## Improved

- Every invoice card now has a clearly labelled **View invoice** button.
- The invoice opens in a secure on-screen preview without downloading first.
- **Download PDF** remains available as a separate action.
- The same preview works for protected Studio Ninja imports and invoices created in BookingSystem2026.
- The main Payments & invoices register now provides View and Download separately.
- The private final telephone-call pack now includes direct invoice access beside the questionnaire and call checklist.

## Safety preserved

- Previewing a Studio Ninja invoice does not send email or enable automation.
- Existing invoice numbers, payments, PDF content and protected imports are unchanged.
- All previous V8.28 fixes remain included.
