# BookingSystem2026 V8.46

## After the Wedding

- A new Journey panel gives every eligible Weddings By Mark job one place to
  track photographs, films, album progress, the optional review request and final
  completion.
- Photograph and film delivery can retain their delivery dates and private working
  links. Album progress moves through image choices, design, supplier order and
  delivery, or can be marked not required.
- The Overview shows compact progress and opens the full panel in one click.

## Protected completion

**Mark wedding complete** is available only when:

1. the wedding date has passed;
2. photographs are delivered;
3. film is delivered or not included;
4. the album is delivered or not included; and
5. the account balance is clear.

The same checks are enforced by the server, so an older screen cannot bypass
them. Completion and reopening retain invoice numbers, payments, accepted quote
history, documents and all delivery progress.

## Review request and safety

- The new review-request template is optional and manual. Mark sees the exact
  recipient, subject and wording and must press Send; saving delivery progress or
  completing a wedding never emails the couple.
- Successful review requests are recorded in the after-wedding panel and Activity.
- Studio Ninja imports can retain private delivery progress but remain manual-only
  for communication.
- Client quotes no longer preselect the first (Bronze) package. The couple must tap
  their intended package before confirming, while Mark's required add-ons remain
  visible, included and protected.
- V8.46 uses the existing booking workflow data, introduces no database migration
  and does not alter invoice or Accounts integration behaviour.
