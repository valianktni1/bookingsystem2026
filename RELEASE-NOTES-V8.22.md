# BookingSystem2026 V8.22 — New Client Updates Dashboard

## What changed

- A prominent **New client updates** card now appears first on the Today screen.
- Newly submitted Wedding Booking Forms and Final Wedding Timings Forms appear there.
- Native agreements signed by the client remain there until countersigned.
- Unread inbox replies appear only when the sender exactly matches the couple's
  primary or partner email address.
- Each item opens directly at the correct client form, agreement or email.
- Booking Form and Final Timings submissions have deliberate **reviewed** actions.

## Safety preserved

- No new automatic client emails are sent.
- Studio Ninja general automation remains suppressed.
- Existing bookings, invoices, documents, agreements and Calendar links are unchanged.
- Accounts synchronisation remains untouched and can stay switched off.
