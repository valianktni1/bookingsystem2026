BookingSystem2026 - Phone Call and Email Controls
Build: 2026.08.06-phone-call-email-controls-v8.9.4

This is a complete cumulative build containing every feature through V8.9.4.

V8.9.4 changes:

- There is one client Wedding Booking Form / questionnaire only.
- The incorrect final-questionnaire waiting stage and client reminder have been removed.
- A private Finalise wedding details by phone task is scheduled for Mark 30 days before each upcoming wedding.
- The phone task sends no client email and does not create another client form.
- Existing imported historical final-details submissions are retained as read-only history.
- Email templates can be added, edited, activated, deactivated and permanently deleted.
- Deleted templates stay deleted after an application restart.
- Active custom templates are available from the individual booking email control.
- Every email sent to an individual client automatically receives a fresh, booking-specific secure account link.
- The account link is appended even when the template does not contain {portal_url}.
- The initial quote email uses Mark's warmer wording and a clear VIEW YOUR FULL QUOTE HERE button.
- Secure client links are displayed as short branded buttons in HTML emails instead of long jumbled addresses.
- Bank details in the quote are taken from Business settings rather than hard-coded into the template.
- Admin-only enquiry notifications and test previews are not given a live client account link.
- Imported Studio Ninja records remain permanently automation-suppressed and receive no automatic emails.

All previous cumulative behaviour is retained, including agreed payment dates, protected archive imports,
original documents and invoice history, packages and add-ons, discounts, client portals, invoices,
payment recording, payment reminders, branded email/PDF output and separate WBM/Ivory Digital numbering.
