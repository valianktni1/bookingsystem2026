BookingSystem2026 - Visible Invoice Void Reasons
Build: 2026.08.12-visible-invoice-void-reasons-v8.9.8.2

This is a complete cumulative build containing every feature through V8.9.8.2.

V8.9.8.2 permanent invoice audit visibility:

- The reason entered when an invoice is voided is displayed directly on its
  retained invoice card in Payments.
- The void date and administrator are displayed with the reason.
- The main Payments & invoices register has a Reason button for every void invoice.
- Existing void reasons are recovered from their retained invoice notes; no
  historical data or invoice numbers are rewritten.
- The retained invoice PDF continues to include the permanent void audit note.

V8.9.8.1 clear client submission:

- The final button now says Submit Wedding Booking Form.
- A large, mobile-friendly success window confirms the form is securely in the
  couple's wedding file and available to Mark.
- The confirmation sends the couple directly to their agreement when they continue.
- Previously submitted forms say updated instead of submitted and remain editable.
- Existing custom wording is preserved; only the original V8.9.8 defaults are upgraded.

V8.9.8 booking form, payment plans and testing:

- Editable Wedding Booking Form with protected workflow questions and custom questions.
- Three mutually exclusive payment plans recalculate native accepted-quote invoices.
- Payment schedule amounts, 90/45-day dates and next unpaid date update automatically.
- Existing payments remain attached and are allocated against the new schedule.
- Studio Ninja invoices, counters and communication suppression remain untouched.
- Safe Testing Mode permanently labels test records and redirects their client emails.
- Real paid bookings retain permanent-deletion protection; TEST journeys can be cleared.

V8.9.7 website form builder:

- A dedicated Web enquiry form page now appears under Setup.
- Mark can edit headings, introduction, payment wording and the thank-you message.
- Questions can be reordered, renamed, shown or hidden and made optional or required.
- Custom short-answer, long-answer, choice-list and tick-box questions can be added,
  edited and deleted from desktop or mobile.
- Essential name, email, date, venue, source and privacy questions are protected so
  an accidental edit cannot stop enquiries creating the correct booking.
- Saving publishes immediately to the existing /enquiry iframe; website code does
  not need to be replaced.
- Every enquiry stores the exact published question wording alongside its answers.
- Original website-enquiry answers are visible inside Forms & agreement on the record.

V8.9.6 mail workspace:

- Live secure IMAP inboxes for Weddings By Mark and Ivory Digital.
- Threaded SMTP replies from the correct business mailbox.
- Incoming senders are matched to their booking or project.
- Attachments, read/unread synchronisation and Hostinger Sent-folder copies.
- Imported Studio Ninja clients stay manual-only with no automatic portal link.

V8.9.5 admin refinement:

- A compact booking command centre replaces the long accordion-based admin record.
- Every record follows the same order: Overview, Quote & emails, Payments,
  Forms & agreement, Files, Notes & history.
- A sticky header keeps Back, Edit, Cancel/Reopen and More actions visible.
- Email, telephone, directions and client-area controls are available immediately.
- Six at-a-glance cards show wedding date, package, outstanding balance,
  payment due date, forms progress and active/void invoice counts.
- The workflow bar follows Enquiry, Quote, Payment, Booking form, Agreement, Wedding.
- The Overview combines client details, wedding details, notes and private tasks.
- Voided invoices are clearly retained and linked from the Overview and Payments screens.
- Cancel booking is a visible top-level action rather than a buried footer control.
- Delete test / duplicate safely removes zero-payment test records, including a voided
  test invoice, while the consumed invoice number remains consumed and is never reused.
- Genuine payments, imported Studio Ninja bookings and retained financial records remain protected.
- Desktop and mobile use the same logical workflow with responsive controls.

All V8.9.4 behaviour remains included: the single Wedding Booking Form, private
30-day phone task, editable email templates, automatic client account links,
friendly quote wording and branded quote button.
