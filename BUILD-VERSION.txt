V8.9.9 cancellation and refund controls:

- Cancel booking & close balance replaces the older status-only cancellation.
- The unpaid part of every retained invoice becomes £0 due without deleting or
  rewriting the original invoice total, number or payment history.
- Payments already received remain recorded as retained income.
- Cancellation date, reason, administrator, retained payment and closed balance

- Open tasks are completed, existing portal links are revoked and all automated
  reminders/client emails stop immediately. Cancellation sends no email.
- Record refund creates a permanent negative payment entry and reduces the amount
  retained; it cannot exceed the money currently retained.
- Cancelled invoices are excluded from outstanding-income totals and due-date lists.
- Reopening safely restores the previous live invoice states and automation setting,
  while revoked client links remain revoked for security.
- Twenty cumulative automated tests pass.
