CHANGES
- Saves the main page, booking panel and table scroll positions on scrolling,
  switching away and page exit. Restores after the route and content load.
- Returning to the root URL in the SAME browser tab resumes the last section.
  Explicit links to other sections/bookings are respected.
- Stops restoring when you start interacting, so you can scroll immediately.
- Clears position memory on logout. Uses bounded session/browser-history data.
- No form answers, email drafts, bank details or booking contents are stored.
- Normal app switching does not re-render or reset the open form.

LIMITS
This is navigation memory, not unsaved-form autosave. If the browser fully
reloads, unsaved edits are not recovered. A newly opened tab or cleared browser
session has no saved position. No authentication bypass or session extension.
No booking records, payments, protected imports or invoices are changed by
this feature. Cumulative package includes the V8.59 native invoice update.
