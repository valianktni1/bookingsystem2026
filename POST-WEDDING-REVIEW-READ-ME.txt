WEDDINGS BY MARK — 14-DAY MARRIED-LIFE, AWARDS AND REVIEW EMAIL

The approved "Hey Guys" email is sent once, 14 days after the wedding, with
County Brides first, Facebook reviews second and the supplied Google review
link third. Both partners are invited to vote separately. The awards wording
includes the approved "second year running" sentence.

The whole County Brides section, including both-partners voting and the deadline,
is omitted for sends after 30 April 2027 in Europe/London. The actual sending
date controls this, not merely the wedding date. Scheduled retries re-render the
email at send time; retries of retained wording remove the approved expired section.
Facebook and Google reviews continue after the cutoff. This is a fixed 2027
campaign; it does not automatically reopen in 2028.

Google: https://g.page/r/CWXyai3xrskCEBM/review
Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/weddingsbymarkmanchester/reviews/
County Brides: https://www.countybrides.com/awards/

The new template is "14-day wedding review & awards request" (post_wedding_review).
It is created safely on existing installations without replacing edited templates.
It uses {county_brides_request} and {review_request_intro} for the date-dependent
paragraphs. Keep these placeholders if editing the master template.
The separate older manual review_request template is retained.

The rule appears in Communications > Workflow Studio under After the wedding.
Default mode is Automatic, 14 days after wedding. Existing Automatic / Review first /
Task only / Off controls remain available. No other workflow defaults are changed.
Manual requests also record the shared review-request status, so an earlier
manual review email prevents the automatic request from duplicating it.
Automatic sends update the after-wedding panel and email audit history.
Deliberately choosing Review & send again still permits a manual resend.

Eligible records: native Weddings By Mark weddings marked confirmed, in progress
or completed, with a wedding date, not archived, cancelled or paused. Existing
Studio Ninja/import protection remains intact. This does not enable bulk email
to old imported records. A previous successful manual or automatic review request
suppresses the automatic request even if the wedding date is later edited.

Delivery uses the EXISTING reminder service: REMINDERS_ENABLED must be true and
SMTP configured. The existing delivery window defaults to 09:00-19:00 UK time,
with the existing scan interval (default six hours). No real emails are sent by
the deployment script or tests. After deployment the background scheduler sends
eligible due emails during the configured window.
The existing seven-day reminder catch-up window applies if the system was down
on the fourteenth day; much older weddings are not bulk-contacted on startup.

No database schema migration is needed. The approved compressed shared awards
graphic, payment references, signatures and quote follow-up exclusions remain.

Validation: 19 new tests cover day 13/day 14, duplicate scans, prior manual sends,
completed and ineligible records, changed dates, existing-template preservation,
the workflow Off control, UK midnight, cutoff dates, later years, SMTP failure,
retry across the cutoff, both MIME alternatives and old retained-body retries.
Regression checks also cover Workflow Studio, custom workflows, manual after-wedding
actions, quote controls, email attachments, signatures and rendering. Three legacy
UI/version assertions fail on the untouched original archive too; these are unrelated
to this change and are not claimed as passing.
