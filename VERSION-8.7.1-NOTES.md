# BookingSystem2026 V8.7.1 - Full Booking Page

V8.7.1 is a cumulative usability correction built on V8.7.

## Full-page jobs

- Clicking a job now opens a complete full-screen booking page instead of a fixed panel on the right.
- The jobs list is no longer visible behind the active booking.
- The whole booking page scrolls normally, including expanded sections.
- A clear **Back to bookings** control returns to the jobs list.
- The expandable job sections remain identical on desktop and mobile.
- Adding a package or optional extra now creates its required internal code automatically from the name, avoiding technical validation errors.
- Packages and add-ons now have Delete controls. Unused duplicates can be removed permanently; anything referenced by an accepted quote or invoice is protected and can be hidden instead.

## Safety

This update changes presentation and navigation only. It does not alter bookings, imported Studio Ninja documents, invoice numbering, payments, reminder schedules, email suppression or Google Calendar settings.
