# BookingSystem2026 V8.8.1 - Payment Dates & Easier Navigation

V8.8.1 is cumulative and includes all V8.8 required add-on and private discount features.

## Payments and invoices

- Every Weddings By Mark final balance date is calculated as exactly 45 days before the wedding.
- Missing dates on imported Studio Ninja invoices are safely derived for display without altering historic records.
- The register includes a clear Final balance due column.
- Overdue balances are highlighted and show how many days overdue they are.
- Outstanding balances are listed first by due date; fully paid and void invoices sit below them.
- No client emails or calendar actions are triggered by this display improvement.

## Full-page record navigation

- A second Back to bookings button now sits beside Email, Call and Directions.
- The existing top-right Back button remains available.
- The shared change applies to weddings, enquiries, imported bookings and Ivory Digital projects.
- Desktop and mobile layouts are both supported.
